## Test dates and registration

The JLPT is usually held twice a year, in July and December. See [JLPT test dates and registration](https://okjlpt.com/en/dates), and always confirm with the official site.

## A simple study plan

1. **Words first, then grammar.** Go through the vocabulary list for your level, then grammar one point at a time.
2. **Take one full past paper** under timed conditions, including listening, to find your weak sections.
3. **Drill by question type.** Practice the sections where you lose the most points, and read the explanation for every question.
4. **Review your mistakes on a schedule.** Bring missed questions back after a few days, not just once.
5. **Take another full paper** a few weeks before the test and estimate your score.

## What OK JLPT does

- **Prep:** flashcards from N5 to N1, and grammar one lesson at a time with a short quiz. Set your exam date and estimate your score.
- **Papers and focused practice:** full past papers by year and level, with listening. In focused practice every question shows its explanation as soon as you answer.
- **Mistake review:** missed questions come back on a spaced schedule, with the words and grammar they test.
- **Notes and AI:** tricks, mnemonics and traps from other test takers under each question. Ask the AI why a sentence takes that answer.
- **Sync:** one account, progress and mistakes sync across iPhone, iPad and the web.

Use it on [the web](https://okjlpt.com/en/papers) or on [iPhone / iPad](https://okjlpt.com/en/download). Pricing: [okjlpt.com/en/pricing](https://okjlpt.com/en/pricing).
